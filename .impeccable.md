## Design Context

### Users
Shinsa is for Pump It Up players, score chasers, live hosts, tournament regulars, and scene friends who check the app throughout the day for social momentum as much as pure utility. They use it to share clears and upscores, jump into live sessions, keep tabs on people they follow, and carry lightweight conversation forward without leaving the game community context.

### Brand Personality
Dark, electric, arcade-social. The product should feel high-energy, expressive, and scene-native rather than corporate or generic. Interfaces should support confidence and playful competitiveness while still feeling warm, readable, and fast.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep Shinsa in its existing dark neon arcade language and borrow interaction patterns from mainstream social apps only where they improve usability. Notes, stories, and message-adjacent surfaces should feel like extensions of Shinsa's current world, not an Instagram skin. Prioritize layered dark surfaces, bright but controlled accents, bold display typography, and interfaces that feel alive without becoming visually noisy.

### Design Principles
1. Preserve Shinsa identity first: borrow behaviors, not aesthetics.
2. Make social activity feel immediate and alive, especially for live sessions, fresh clears, and new upscores.
3. Keep dense interaction surfaces readable on mobile with strong hierarchy and clear touch targets.
4. Let expressive elements like stickers and story cards add personality without obscuring the core content.
5. Treat competitive activity as celebration, not sterile data entry: scores and clears should feel shareable and dramatic.
